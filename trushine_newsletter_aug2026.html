⚠ Grease Trap Alert — Southeast Operators

Florida enforcement is tightening. FOG violations now carry fines up to $10,000 per day for water quality issues, and a second offense grease disposal violation in Florida can result in a 12-month license revocation. Nashville, Charlotte, and Atlanta are all active enforcement markets — meaning inspectors conduct routine compliance checks, not just complaint-driven ones.

The most common health inspection violations in 2026: grease buildup, pest activity, dirty food-contact surfaces, and missing compliance records. That last one is avoidable — and it's what we eliminate for every client with our digital CASR reporting system.

💡 What Inspectors Are Looking For

FOG inspections are often conducted by wastewater inspectors — a completely different agency from your health department. Many operators pass health inspections but get hit by utility compliance fines they never saw coming. If you don't have documented pump-out records on file, you are at risk regardless of how clean your kitchen looks.
