Columbus kitchens

Columbus lines have to be cold before the floor is honest

Broadway kitchens often close in a small room with one way out. The crew needs that close to be real. If the last pickup is still on the pass, the deep clean becomes a wipe, and we will not rename a wipe. Wynnton and Midtown conversions have floors that were not poured as quarry tile. Soil hides in the seams.

Victory Drive and Veterans Parkway boxes have the opposite problem. There is room, the equipment is heavier, and nobody rolls a fryer unless the night was planned. Northside walk-ins collect shelf soil that a lunch mop never touches. Kitchens that swing with the Fort Moore calendar may want the reset on a specific week. Say which week.

Photos show the areas we could reach. Equipment that will not move stays in the note. The fan on the roof is the hood page.

Who inspects

A deep clean does not replace the inspector

Columbus Fire and EMS and the environmental health office look at different risks. A cleaner floor can make a walkthrough easier. It does not issue their result, and it does not renew a suppression tag.

General guidance only. No local code citation. Phenix City is not covered by this city page.

If the canopy, the duct, or the fan is the item that is due, book the Columbus hood page. A deep clean stops at the room.

The visit

What a Columbus kitchen deep clean includes

Floors and drains

Cookline floors, corners, and reachable drains once the appliances are off. Uptown seams and expressway tile are both in scope.

Under the equipment

What rolls, gets cleaned under. What is bolted down is named in the note.

Walls and shelves

Splash zones and walk-in shelves. Not a dining-room detail clean unless that was the room you booked.

Not the hood or trap

Exhaust cleaning and interceptor pumping are the other Columbus pages.

Questions

Columbus deep cleaning questions

Does this include hood cleaning?

No. Columbus hood cleaning is a separate page and a separate crew task.

Do you need the restaurant closed?

The kitchen line has to be off. The dining room schedule is yours to set. Tell us when the appliances can be shut down.

Will this pass health inspection?

It removes soil. It is not a score and not a substitute for the environmental health visit.

How far is Columbus from the shop?

It is a scheduled I-185 run from Norcross. We respond to the request the same business day. The truck is not already downtown.
