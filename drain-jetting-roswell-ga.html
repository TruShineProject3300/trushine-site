Roswell drain jetting FAQ

Why do Roswell kitchens need drain jetting?

Grease and food solids coat kitchen drain lines until they back up — an instant health violation and often a mid-service shutdown. Jetting scours the full line, not just the clog snaking reaches.

How often should lines be jetted?

High-volume kitchens benefit from quarterly jetting; most kitchens every six months. TruShine sets the cadence to your line condition and volume.

How is jetting different from snaking?

Snaking punches a hole through a clog; high-pressure jetting scours the full pipe diameter back to bare wall, removing the grease layer that causes repeat backups.
