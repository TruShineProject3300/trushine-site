Montgomery Service Area

Pressure washing for the Montgomery commercial mix

Montgomery's long, hot summers and humid air help mildew and algae grow on shaded concrete, walls, and walkways. Restaurants and retail along the city's commercial corridors also deal with grease, gum, and drive-thru grime. Regular washing keeps those surfaces from building up stains that are much harder to remove later.

Spring pollen leaves a yellow-green film on storefronts, signs, and sidewalks, and if it stays wet it can feed mildew. A wash after peak pollen season resets the exterior. Wet weather keeps north-facing walls and shaded concrete damp, and organic growth follows. It looks bad and can be slippery underfoot.

We also wash commercial property in Prattville, Millbrook, Wetumpka, and Pike Road, so businesses with several locations in the Montgomery area can keep one exterior cleaning schedule.

Grease Control

The dirtiest square footage on the property

Restaurants carry grease out on shoes, carts, and trash bags, and it settles into the concrete outside the back door and around the dumpster. Left alone, it turns black, smells, attracts pests, and becomes slippery. Degreasing those areas regularly keeps them manageable.

Safety

Safety, pests, and first impressions

Grease and food residue outside attract flies, rodents, and other pests, and slick concrete is a fall risk. Pressure washing addresses both, and it makes your storefront look the way you want customers to remember it.

Frequency

Choosing the right frequency

For most Montgomery restaurants, back-of-house concrete and dumpster areas benefit from recurring service, while the front of the property can be handled less often. We set separate intervals so each area gets what it needs.

Wastewater

Where the dirty water goes

It is easy to make a dumpster pad look clean by hosing everything toward the nearest drain, and that is exactly the practice stormwater rules are designed to stop. TruShine contains and recovers wash water so your property is not the source of a discharge complaint.

Method

Pressure is not the whole story

We protect what is around the surface too: landscaping, signage, windows, and doors. Choosing the right nozzle, pressure, and spray direction keeps the cleaning on the target and away from things that could be damaged.

Questions

Montgomery pressure washing questions

Can you wash before or after business hours?

Yes. Most Montgomery work is scheduled when the property is closed or quiet, so customers and staff are not disrupted.

Do you clean kitchen floors and floor mats?

Yes. Kitchen floors and rubber mats collect grease that ordinary mopping struggles with, and they can be included in your Montgomery scope.

Why do Montgomery restaurants need pressure washing?

Grease, food waste, and spills collect on back-door concrete, dumpster pads, and drive-thrus. Over time they create odors, attract pests, and become slippery. Regular washing keeps those areas under control.

Is this a one-time service or recurring?

Either. Many businesses start with a one-time cleanup and then move to a recurring schedule for high-traffic areas.
