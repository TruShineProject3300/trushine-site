Frequency

How often Triangle kitchens need hood cleaning

NFPA 96 sets cleaning frequency by what and how much you cook. Solid fuel cooking: monthly. High-volume operations such as 24-hour kitchens, charbroiling, or wok cooking: quarterly. Moderate volume: semi-annually. Low volume, such as some churches or seasonal kitchens: annually.

Fire inspectors in Wake, Durham, and Orange counties look for a current label on the hood, and North Carolina posts a sanitation grade from the county health department. A heavy grease load hurts you on both fronts. We set the schedule, remind you before it is due, and keep the records.
