Concord kitchens

Concord has a square and a highway, and the oil sits differently

Union Street and the blocks just off it were not designed for a pump truck at dinner. The container has to come out before the lot fills, or after the dining room closes. Branchview and the older commercial corners have the same constraint: one driveway, and the dumpster already lives there.

Bruton Smith Boulevard, the outlet and speedway area, and George W. Liles Parkway were built for trucks. The failure there is a tank that was fine on a normal Tuesday and full when an event weekend hits. Poplar Tent and the Kannapolis edge add more of those pads. Tell us if your volume spikes on event weeks. We will not invent a schedule from a map.

Concord already has hood, grease trap, and deep cleaning pages. Oil pickup does not satisfy the Concord Fire Department, and it does not satisfy Cabarrus Health Alliance. Those are different files.

Who inspects

Concord inspections this pickup does not replace

The Concord Fire Department is the fire authority for cooking exhaust inside the city. Cabarrus Health Alliance handles environmental health for the county, including the food side of the kitchen. Used oil is a third subject.

General guidance only. No ordinance number on this page. If the address is in Harrisburg, or just over the line in Kannapolis, confirm which fire marshal covers it before you argue about a sticker.

Event weekends change how fast a Concord fryer fills a tank. A quiet Monday pickup can still be the right plan. It is not the right plan for every pad on Bruton Smith Boulevard.

The visit

What a Concord oil pickup includes

The container on that pad

Caddies off Union Street and tanks on the boulevard are both in scope. The record names the container we actually emptied.

Event-week timing

If the tank only overflows on event weekends, say so. A standing Tuesday route is the wrong tool for that kitchen.

Pad condition

Oil on the kitchen's own concrete is part of the visit. We write down what was still there if the pad could not be finished.

A separate ticket

Date and amount only. Not a hood report. Not a trap manifest. Concord keeps those on their own pages.

Service area

Concord areas where the container usually sits

These are pickup geographies, not a client list.

Union Street

The downtown square and the lots behind it. Short approaches and shared dumpsters.

Bruton Smith Boulevard

Large pads, including the speedway and outlet side of town. Tanks, and weekends that do not look like Tuesdays.

George W. Liles and Poplar Tent

Newer retail fronts. The pad is planned, and the tank is easy to forget until it is full.

Branchview and the Kannapolis line

Older commercial corners, then the city line. Harrisburg is the other direction and has its own address.

A practical benefit

What you can show after the stop

A date and an amount, and a pad that is not holding last week's oil. That is the benefit. It does not pass an inspection by itself.

If one Concord store fries all day and another barely fries, do not put them on one pickup. The drive from Winston-Salem is the same. The containers are not.

Questions

Concord used oil questions

Is this the Concord grease trap service?

No. The trap page is separate. This page is fryer oil.

Does Cabarrus Health Alliance collect the oil?

No. Cabarrus Health Alliance is the environmental health authority. The pickup is a service visit with its own record.

Can pickup move on speedway or event weekends?

Yes. Tell us the weeks the tank actually fills. A fixed day that misses those weekends is the wrong plan.

Do you cover Kannapolis on this page?

Kannapolis is the next city and has its own hood page. Give the Concord address if the kitchen is in Concord.
