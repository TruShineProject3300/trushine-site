Hood, filters, and plenum

Filters come out and get washed. The canopy shell, grease troughs, and the plenum behind the filters are scraped and degreased. A wipe of the face of the hood is not a finished exhaust cleaning.

Ductwork and access panels

Duct cleaning stops at openings that are really there. The crew opens the panels that exist and leaves them so they can be opened again. A missing or painted-shut panel is a limit on the report, not a section that was cleaned.

Rooftop exhaust fan

When the roof is safe to work, the fan is hinged and the wheel and housing are cleaned. A hinge that is seized is reported, not forced until something breaks. A bright canopy with a still-loaded fan is an unfinished job.

Certificate sticker and report

The sticker on the hood names TruShine Services and the service date. The written report lists what was opened and what could not be reached. The sticker is not a suppression tag, and it is not a certification of the kitchen.

Questions

Greensboro hood cleaning FAQ

What does NFPA 96 compliant hood cleaning include in Greensboro?

An inspection of the exhaust path, then cleaning of the hood, the filters, the ductwork that can be opened, the access panels that exist, and the rooftop exhaust fan when the roof can be worked. You leave with a post-service report and a certificate sticker. That package is NFPA 96 compliant. It is not a suppression tag.

How often should a Greensboro kitchen have the hood cleaned?

NFPA 96 ties the clock to the cooking, not to the ZIP code. Kitchens burning solid fuel are on the short interval, often every month. Lines that charbroil, run a wok, or stay open around the clock are often on a three month interval. A typical restaurant line is often every six months. Seasonal and low volume cooking is often once a year. Grease on the hood, filters, duct, or fan is the reason to clean, and the fire authority at that address can shorten the clock.

Who is the fire authority in Greensboro, and who is it in the rest of the Triad?

Inside Greensboro city limits, start with the Greensboro Fire Department. High Point, Winston-Salem, Kernersville, and the other nearby cities govern themselves. A health inspection is a different visit. Name the city when you book so the report is not written as if one fire marshal covers the whole Triad.

Does this visit replace the suppression inspection?

No. This visit removes grease from the hood, filters, ductwork, and rooftop exhaust fan. The wet chemical system over the cookline is a different appointment, with a different tag. Cleaning the hood leaves that tag where it was.
