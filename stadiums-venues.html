What We Do at Venues

Eight Compliance Services Built for Venue Operations

Every service is engineered for the demands venues face — multiple kitchens, concurrent events, event-day inspections, and facilities teams that answer to both the fire marshal and the brand.

🔥

Hood Cleaning & Repair

NFPA 96 compliant exhaust system cleaning. Full duct-to-fan degreasing across every kitchen. Before/after photo documentation for your fire marshal and insurance carrier. Emergency and after-hours available for event scheduling.

🧯

Fire Suppression Inspection

Brand-certified inspection for Ansul, Amerex, Badger, Pyrochem, Buckeye, and Captive Aire systems. Semi-annual inspection with digital documentation. Every kitchen in your venue covered under one contract.

Grease Trap Service

Full pump-out, cleaning, and FOG compliance documentation for traps from 50 gallons to 1,500+ gallons. Scheduled around your event calendar — never during critical operational hours.

🧹

Deep Cleaning — BOH & FOH

Full kitchen deep cleaning: equipment, walls, floors, walk-ins, prep surfaces, and FOH dining areas. Venue-grade turnaround between events. Bilingual crews.

Commercial Pest Control

Integrated Pest Management across every kitchen, concession, and food service area. Monthly service with documented reports. Emergency callback included — because a pest sighting on event day is not an option.

💨

Pressure Washing

Dumpster pads, loading docks, concourses, parking decks, rooftop hood areas, and building exteriors. Venue-grade pressure washing with event-aware scheduling.

UCO Management & Fry360 Oil Filtration

Used cooking oil collection with IoT monitoring. Fry360 oil filtration service extends oil life by 60% — reducing oil costs and fire risk across every fryer in your venue. Currently managing 100+ fryers at major Atlanta venues.

High-pressure hydro jetting for kitchen drains, floor drains, and grease lines. Camera inspection for persistent issues. A drain backup on event day shuts down a kitchen — we prevent that.

Frequently Asked Questions

Venue Compliance Questions

How do you handle multiple kitchens in one venue?

We assign a dedicated account manager to your venue. They coordinate scheduling across all kitchens, maintain the compliance dashboard, and serve as your single point of contact. One call handles everything.

Can you work around our event schedule?

That's what we're built for. We have your event calendar integrated into our scheduling system. All services are completed during approved windows — never during load-in, events, or load-out unless specifically requested for emergency response.

What happens if we have an emergency on event day?

We maintain an on-call crew for venue clients. Emergency response is included in your compliance program — no separate emergency fees for contracted venues. We mobilize within hours, not days.

How is venue pricing structured?

Venue pricing is always a flat monthly or annual contract based on scope — number of kitchens, service frequency, and event schedule. We never bill hourly at venues. You know exactly what you're paying every month.

Which states do you cover?

Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, Florida, Virginia, and Alabama. If your venue portfolio spans multiple states, one contract covers them all.
