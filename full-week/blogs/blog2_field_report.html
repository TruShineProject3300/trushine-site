Why Documentation Is the Product

The single most important shift in commercial kitchen compliance in 2026: inspectors increasingly review records before they assess physical conditions in the kitchen. Documentation is now as important as operational hygiene. A spotless kitchen can still receive a critical violation because a certificate is missing, incomplete, or doesn't meet the 2025 NFPA 96 standard.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a commercial kitchen compliance company do overnight?
Hood cleaning, fryer service, oil filtration, grease trap inspection, and full digital documentation, all completed between service hours. Every location receives a CASR report with before/after photos, supervisor name, time in/out, and compliance status before the crew leaves.
How is a CASR report generated?
Through a field inspection tablet in every service van. The technician documents each fryer and hood individually, photographs before and after states, logs oil by weight, and submits from the field. It auto-delivers to designated client contacts before the crew leaves.
What is the NFPA 96 documentation requirement?
Under the 2025 update, every cleaning following NFPA 96 must produce a digital certificate with date, company name, technician credentials, areas serviced, and timestamped before/after photos. A sticker on the hood no longer meets the standard.
How quickly can a new kitchen be onboarded?
Walk the kitchen Day 1, assess gaps, same-day proposal, follow up within 24 hours, begin service within the same week once signed. Four new clients were signed and serviced within a single week in this report.
What makes documentation inspection-ready?
service records following NFPA 96 on file and accessible on premises, grease trap manifests covering 3 years retained at the location, fire suppression certificates within 6 months, and licensed UCO chain-of-custody records, all producible within 60 seconds.
