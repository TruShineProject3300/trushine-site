Frequently Asked Questions — Atlanta

How often does a commercial kitchen hood need to be cleaned in Atlanta?
Under NFPA 96, hood cleaning in Atlanta depends on cooking volume and fuel type. Solid fuel operations require monthly cleaning. High-volume operations and any kitchen running more than 16 hours per day (new 2025 requirement) require quarterly cleaning minimum. Low-volume operations may qualify for semi-annual cleaning. Every cleaning must produce digital documentation with timestamps and before/after photos — a sticker on the hood is no longer sufficient under the 2025 NFPA 96 update.
How often does a grease trap need to be pumped in Georgia?
Atlanta Department of Watershed Management enforces FOG compliance in Atlanta independently from the health department — these are two different agencies that do not coordinate. Most Georgia jurisdictions require grease trap pumping every 90 days maximum. All pump-out manifests must be retained on premises for 3 years minimum. You can pass your health inspection and fail your FOG inspection on the same day.
What documentation does TruShine provide after each service in Atlanta?
Every TruShine service generates a CASR (Cleaning and Service Record) report with per-fryer documentation, before/after photos with timestamps, supervisor name and certification, service scope, and compliance notes. The report delivers automatically to your designated contacts before our crew leaves your building. Records are retained for 3 years and are immediately accessible for any inspection.

Yes. TruShine operates across all of Georgia and 6 additional southeastern states. Multi-location operators receive one compliance calendar covering all locations, one documentation system, one account manager, and one invoice.
