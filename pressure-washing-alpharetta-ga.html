Alpharetta Service Area

Planning exterior work in Alpharetta

Alpharetta properties tend to be newer construction with light-colored concrete, pavers, and stone or stucco facades that show dirt quickly. Outdoor dining is common in the city's walkable districts, so patios and sidewalks collect food spills, drink residue, and gum. North Fulton's heavy tree cover also drops pollen and leaf stains onto entries every spring and fall.

Spring pollen leaves a yellow-green film on storefronts, signs, and sidewalks, and if it stays wet it can feed mildew. A wash after peak pollen season resets the exterior. Red clay is one of the most stubborn stains on light concrete. It responds best to treatment before it has baked in through a few hot weeks.

Along with Alpharetta, we serve Milton, Johns Creek, Roswell, and Cumming. One call can cover every location you run in metro Atlanta.

Keeping wash water out of storm drains

A pressure washing job is only done right if the dirty water is handled too. Grease, food waste, and detergent should not end up in the storm system around Alpharetta. We block and recover wash water as part of the job instead of treating it as an afterthought.

Grease Control

Restaurant service areas

Grease does not stay in the kitchen. It works its way out to the back door, the trash area, and sometimes the drive-thru lane. A proper degreaser removes what plain water cannot, and regular service keeps it from building into permanent staining.

Frequency

Recurring or one-time?

There is no single right frequency. Restaurants often wash dumpster pads and back-door concrete on a recurring schedule, while storefronts and sidewalks may only need attention seasonally. We look at your property and recommend a plan you can adjust.

Safety

Safety, pests, and first impressions

Clean entries and sidewalks shape how customers see a business before they walk in. Grime and gum suggest neglect, while clean concrete and glass signal a well-run property. Removing grease and organic growth also cuts down on slips and pests.
